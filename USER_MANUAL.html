Chapter 1

Introduction

This chapter explains what EduGrant is, the programs it manages, and how to open the system in a web browser.

1.1 System Overview

EduGrant is the online scholarship management platform of the Community Affairs and Youth Development Office (CAYDO) of Carmona, Cavite. It allows residents to apply for scholarship or financial-assistance grants from any device with an internet connection and lets CAYDO staff review and process those applications without paper forms.

Key capabilities include:

  • Online application submission with document uploads
  • Automated eligibility screening
  • Real-time application status tracking with email notifications
  • Staff tools for reviewing documents and updating application status
  • Program management and analytics/reporting
Figure 1 · The EduGrant public homepage at https://edugrant-self.vercel.app/ — visitors can learn about available programs and begin the registration process.

1.2 Scholarship Programs

ProgramFull NameDescription
SPARK Support for Professionals and Aspiring Residents with Knowledge One-time financial assistance for Carmona residents preparing for a licensure, bar, or board examination.
SINAG Scholarship Incentive for Nationals Achieving Greatness Academic scholarship for Carmona residents currently enrolled in college or a technical-vocational program.

1.3 Accessing the System

Open any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, or Safari) and navigate to:

System URL https://edugrant-self.vercel.app/

No software installation is required. An internet connection and a valid email address are the only prerequisites for applicants.

Chapter 2

Account Setup

Before submitting an application, every resident must create a personal account and verify their email address. This chapter walks through registration, OTP verification, and login.

2.1 Creating an Account

From the homepage, click the Register or Get Started button to open the account-creation form.

Figure 2 · The registration form. Fill in your full name, a valid email address, and a password.
  1. Enter your First Name and Last Name.
  2. Enter a valid Email Address — this will be used for login and notifications. Make sure you have access to this inbox.
  3. Create a Password (minimum 8 characters) and re-enter it in the confirmation field.
  4. Click Register. The system will send a one-time password (OTP) to your email.
Tip Use an email address you check regularly. All status update notifications from CAYDO will be sent there.

2.2 Email Verification (OTP)

After registration, you are redirected to the OTP verification page. Check your email inbox for a message from EduGrant containing a 6-digit code.

Figure 3 · The OTP verification page. Enter the 6-digit code sent to your registered email address.
  1. Open your email and locate the message with the subject "EduGrant — Verify Your Account".
  2. Copy the 6-digit OTP from the email.
  3. Paste or type the code into the verification field on the screen.
  4. Click Verify. Once confirmed, your account is activated.
Note The OTP expires after a limited time. If it expires before you enter it, use the Resend Code link on the verification page to request a new one.

2.3 Logging In

Once your account is verified, you can log in at any time.

Figure 4 · The login page. Enter your registered email address and password.
  1. Navigate to https://edugrant-self.vercel.app/ and click Login.
  2. Enter your Email Address and Password.
  3. Click Login. You will be taken to the applicant home page.
Chapter 3

Applicant Guide

This chapter covers everything an applicant needs to know — browsing programs, filling out the application form, uploading documents, and tracking the status of a submission.

3.1 Applicant Home Page

After logging in, you land on the applicant home page. Here you can see the available CAYDO scholarship programs and quickly navigate to apply or view your current applications.

Figure 5 · The logged-in applicant home page, showing SPARK and SINAG program cards and navigation links in the header.

The top navigation bar includes links to:

  • Home — return to this page
  • Programs — view all active scholarship programs
  • My Applications — see the status of your submissions
  • My Profile — manage your account information

3.2 Viewing Available Programs

Click Programs in the navigation bar to see all currently active programs offered by CAYDO.

3.4 Submitting an Application — Step 2: Document Upload

Step 2 requires you to upload supporting documents. The required documents depend on the program you selected. Common requirements include:

  • Filled-out Application Form
  • Certificate of Graduation
  • Transcript of Records
  • Valid Government-Issued ID (front and back)
  • Original Barangay Clearance
  • Original Barangay Indigency
Figure 8 · Application Step 2 — Document Upload. Click "Choose File" next to each requirement and select the appropriate file from your device.
  1. For each document requirement listed, click Choose File and select the corresponding file from your device.
  2. Accepted file types are PDF, JPG, and PNG only. Files larger than the allowed size limit will be rejected.
  3. After uploading all required documents, click Submit Application.
  4. A confirmation message appears on-screen once your application has been successfully submitted. You will also receive an email notification.
Important Only PDF, JPG, and PNG files are accepted. Attempting to upload other file types (e.g., Word documents, ZIP files) will show an error. Scan or photograph your physical documents and save them in one of the accepted formats before uploading.

3.5 My Applications

Click My Applications in the navigation bar to see all applications you have submitted or have in progress.

Each row in the table shows:

  • A View Details link to open the full application record

Chapter 4

Profile Management

Applicants can update their email address and change their password from the My Profile page.

4.1 Viewing Your Profile

Click My Profile in the top navigation bar to open your account settings page.

Figure 11 · The My Profile page. Your registered email address is displayed, with options to change your email or password.

The Profile page shows:

  • Your current registered email address
  • A Change Email button
  • A Change Password button

4.2 Changing Your Email Address

To change the email address linked to your account, click Change Email on your Profile page.

Figure 12 · The Change Email page. Enter your new email address and confirm it. A verification OTP will be sent to the new address.
  1. Click Change Email on the My Profile page.
  2. Enter your new email address in the New Email field.
  3. Re-enter the address in the Confirm New Email field.
  4. Click Update Email. An OTP will be sent to the new address.
  5. Enter the OTP on the verification page to confirm the change.
Important After changing your email address, future login attempts and notifications will use the new address. Make sure the new email is active and accessible before confirming the change.

4.3 Changing Your Password

  1. On the My Profile page, click Change Password.
  2. Enter your Current Password to confirm your identity.
  3. Enter a New Password (at least 8 characters) and re-enter it in the confirmation field.
  4. Click Update Password. Your new password takes effect immediately.
Chapter 5

Staff Guide — Applications & Applicants

This chapter is for CAYDO staff members who review, approve, or reject scholarship applications. Staff accounts have elevated access to all applicant data and administrative tools.

5.1 Staff Login

The staff portal uses a separate login page to keep administrative access distinct from the applicant portal.

Figure 13 · The staff login page. Enter your assigned staff username and password to access the administrative portal.
  1. Open your browser and go to https://edugrant-self.vercel.app/staff/login.
  2. Enter your Username and Password as provided by your system administrator.
  3. Click Login. You will be redirected to the Admin Dashboard.
Note Staff accounts are created by the system administrator. If you do not have login credentials, contact your office IT officer.

5.2 Admin Dashboard

The Admin Dashboard is the central hub for monitoring scholarship activity at a glance.

Figure 14 · The Admin Dashboard. Summary stat cards appear at the top, followed by a table of recent applications and a sidebar with status and program breakdowns.

The dashboard shows:

  • Summary Cards — Total Applicants, Total Applications, Pending Applications, Approved Applications
  • Recent Applications — latest submissions with applicant name, program, date, and status
  • Status Breakdown — count of applications per status (sidebar)
  • Program Breakdown — count of applications per program (sidebar)

5.3 Applicants List

The Applicants page shows every registered applicant in the system along with their profile information.

Figure 15 · The Applicants list. Use the search bar at the top to filter by name or email.

The table columns include: Full Name, Email, Barangay, Date Registered, and Number of Applications. The search bar allows real-time filtering by name or email address.

5.4 Applications List

The Applications page is the primary queue for tracking and managing all application submissions across all programs.

Figure 16 · The Applications list. Filter by program and status, then click "Review" on any application to open the full review page.

Available filters at the top of the page:

  • Program — filter by SPARK, SINAG, or all programs
  • Status — filter by Draft, Submitted, In Review, Awaiting Physical, Approved, or Rejected

The Screening column shows whether the automated eligibility check passed or failed based on the rules configured for that program.

5.5 Reviewing an Application

Click Review on any application in the Applications list to open the full Application Review page. This is where staff verify documents, update document statuses, and make a final decision on the application.

Figure 17a · Application Review — top section showing the applicant's personal and educational information, status timeline, and document checklist.
Figure 17b · Application Review — bottom section with document status dropdowns, View File links, the Admin Actions panel, and the Status Guide.

Reviewing Documents

Each document requirement has:

  • A View File link to open the uploaded document in a new tab
  • A status dropdown to mark the document as Submitted, Invalid, or Missing
  • An Issue Notes field to leave a comment (included in the notification email if marked Invalid)
  1. Click View File next to a document to open and inspect it.
  2. If the document is acceptable, leave the status as Submitted.
  3. If the document is unreadable, incorrect, or expired, set the status to Invalid and enter an explanation in the Issue Notes field.
  4. Click Update to save the document status. The applicant will receive an email if a document is marked Invalid.

Admin Actions

The Admin Actions panel on the right side of the page contains buttons to update the overall application status:

Action ButtonSets Status ToWhen to Use
Awaiting PhysicalAwaiting PhysicalOnline documents are acceptable; applicant must now bring physical originals to the CAYDO office.
Fully ApproveApprovedAll requirements met; application is granted.
In ReviewIn ReviewMark the application as actively being reviewed while staff conduct further checks.
RejectRejectedApplication does not meet requirements or documents are unsatisfactory.
Important Status changes trigger an automatic email notification to the applicant. Double-check your decision before clicking any action button, as status updates are logged and cannot be undone through the web interface.

Automated Eligibility Screening

Immediately after an application is submitted (and again whenever a document status changes), the system's rule engine evaluates the application against the eligibility criteria defined for the program. The result is shown in the Screening Result section of the review page, indicating which rules passed and which failed.

Chapter 6

Staff Guide — Programs & System Tools

This chapter covers program management, adding new programs and document requirements, generating reports, and managing archived records.

6.1 Managing Programs

Click Programs in the staff navigation bar to view all programs currently in the system.

Figure 18 · The admin Programs list. Each program shows its status (Active/Archived) and action buttons for Edit, Archive, and Delete.

Each program card shows:

  • Program name and description
  • Current status (Active or Archived)
  • Edit — modify program details and document requirements
  • Archive — hide the program from applicants without deleting it
  • Delete — permanently remove the program (use with caution)
Caution Archiving a program prevents new applications from being submitted but preserves all existing application data. Deleting a program is irreversible and removes all associated data.

6.2 Adding a New Program

Click Add New Program at the top of the Programs page to create a new scholarship or grant program.

Figure 19 · The Add New Program form. Fill in the program details at the top, then add document requirements in the formset below.

Document Requirements

The form includes a dynamic list of document requirement rows. For each required document:

6.3 Reports & Analytics

Click Reports in the navigation bar to open the Reports & Analytics page.

Figure 20 · The Reports & Analytics page with summary stat cards, a filter bar, an Applications Over Time bar chart, and per-program breakdowns.

Summary Statistics

The four stat cards at the top show counts for: Total Applications, Approved Applications, Pending Applications, and Registered Applicants.

Date Filtering

6.4 Viewing and Printing Reports

Each report opens on a dedicated, print-optimized page with a purple header and a formatted data table.

Figure 21 · The Applications Report view. The purple header shows the report title and "As of [date]". Use the toolbar buttons to print or download the report.

The report toolbar (visible on-screen but hidden when printing) provides:

  • Back — return to the Reports & Analytics page (the active filter is preserved)
  • Download CSV — download the report data as a spreadsheet file
  • Print / Save as PDF — opens the browser's print dialog; select Save as PDF to save a digital copy
Printing Tip In the print dialog, set the paper size to A4 Landscape and margins to Default or Minimum for the best results. The report is pre-formatted to fit A4 landscape at full width.

6.5 Archived Items

The Archived Items page stores programs and applications that have been removed from active circulation but are retained for record-keeping.

Figure 22 · The Archived Items page. Archived Programs appear in the top section and Archived Applications in the bottom section. Both sections display "No archived items" when nothing has been archived yet.

Items in the archive can be:

  • Restored — returned to active status
  • Permanently Deleted — removed entirely from the database
Appendix A

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: I did not receive the OTP email. What should I do?

First, check your Spam or Junk folder. If it is not there, click the Resend Code link on the verification page. Make sure the email address you registered with is correct and active. If the problem persists, contact the CAYDO office.

Q: Can I edit my application after submitting it?

No. Once an application is submitted, its status changes from Draft and can no longer be edited through the portal. If you need to make changes, contact the CAYDO office. Staff can request you to resubmit documents if any are marked Invalid.

Q: I uploaded a document but it shows as "Missing." Why?

This can happen if the file upload did not complete successfully. Check the Application Detail page for any notes from staff. If the document shows as Invalid, a corrected version may be requested through the CAYDO office.

Q: Can I apply to both SPARK and SINAG at the same time?

Yes, applications for different programs are handled separately. You may submit one application per program at a time. Make sure you meet the eligibility criteria for both programs before applying.

Q: My file upload shows an error saying the file type is not allowed.

The system only accepts PDF, JPG, and PNG files. Convert your document to one of these formats using a scanner app, a phone camera (save as JPG or PNG), or an online file converter, then try uploading again.

Q: How long does it take for my application to be reviewed?

Processing times depend on CAYDO office capacity and the volume of applications received. You will receive an email notification whenever your application status changes. You can also check the My Applications page at any time for an update.

Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the login page, click Forgot Password. Enter your registered email address and follow the instructions in the reset email to set a new password.

Q: (Staff) How do I create a new staff account?

Staff accounts are managed through the Django admin panel. Only superusers (system administrators) can create or modify staff accounts. Contact the system administrator assigned to EduGrant for assistance.

Q: (Staff) The CSV download shows garbled characters. How do I fix this?

Open Microsoft Excel, go to Data → From Text/CSV, select the downloaded file, and set the encoding to UTF-8. This is a common issue with CSV files containing Filipino currency characters and is not a system error.
