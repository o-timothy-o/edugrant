About EduGrant

Community Affairs and Youth Development Office – Carmona, Cavite

Who We Are

The Community Affairs and Youth Development Office (CAYDO) is a local government office under the Municipality of Carmona, Cavite. CAYDO is responsible for administering youth development programs, community welfare initiatives, and scholarship grants for qualified Carmona residents.

EduGrant is CAYDO's online scholarship management system, designed to streamline the application process for the SPARK and SINAG programs. It allows residents to submit and track their applications entirely online, while enabling CAYDO staff to manage, review, and process submissions efficiently.

SPARK

Specialized Program & Advancing Review Knowledge

A one-time financial assistance grant for Carmona residents preparing for professional licensure, board, or bar examinations.

Who Can Apply?

  • Must be a resident of Carmona, Cavite
  • Must have completed a degree program
  • Must be preparing for a PRC board, licensure, or bar examination
  • One-time assistance only — cannot be availed again after approval

Document Requirements

  • Filled-out application form
  • Copy of Certificate of Graduation
  • Photocopy of Transcript of Records
  • Photocopy of valid government-issued ID (front and back)
  • Original Barangay Clearance
  • Original Barangay Indigency

All physical copies of requirements must be submitted on-site at the CAYDO office after online filing. Submitting an application does not guarantee approval.

SINAG

Scholarship, Incentives & Networking in Advancing Graduate Studies

An ongoing scholarship program for Carmona residents pursuing Master's, Doctorate, Law, Medicine, or other advanced degree programs.

Who Can Apply?

  • Must be a resident of Carmona, Cavite
  • Must be enrolled in a graduate or advanced degree program
  • Must meet academic performance requirements set by CAYDO
  • Assistance is not renewable for students repeating a program year

Document Requirements

  • Filled-out application form
  • Copy of Certificate of Graduation (undergraduate)
  • Photocopy of Transcript of Records
  • Photocopy of valid government-issued ID (front and back)
  • Original Barangay Clearance
  • Original Barangay Indigency

Applications are reviewed per batch. You may re-apply after a previous application has been resolved. Submitting an application does not guarantee approval.
