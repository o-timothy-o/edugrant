Specialized Program & Advancing Review Knowledge

Who can apply?

SPARK
Specialized Program and Review Knowledge

A one-time grant assistance program administered by CAYDO for Carmona residents preparing for professional licensure, board, or bar examinations.

Who can apply?
  • Must be a resident of Carmona, Cavite
  • Must have completed a degree program
  • Must be preparing for a PRC board, licensure, or bar examination
  • One-time assistance only — cannot be availed again after approval
Document Requirements
  • Filled out application form
  • Copy of Certificate of Graduation
  • Photocopy of Transcript of Records
  • Photocopy of valid government-issued ID (back to back)
  • Original Barangay Clearance
  • Original Barangay Indigency
Important Notes

Applications are reviewed by CAYDO administrators. Submitting an application does not guarantee approval. All physical copies of requirements must be submitted on-site after online filing.

SINAG
Scholarship, Incentives and Networking in Achieving Graduate Studies

A scholarship assistance program administered by CAYDO for Carmona residents pursuing Master's, Doctorate, Law, or other advanced degree programs.

Who can apply?
  • Must be a resident of Carmona, Cavite
  • Must be enrolled in a graduate or advanced degree program (Master's, Doctorate, Law, Medicine, etc.)
  • Must meet academic performance requirements set by CAYDO
  • Applicants are expected to pass their program — assistance is not renewable for repeating students
Document Requirements
  • Filled out application form
  • Copy of Certificate of Graduation (undergraduate)
  • Photocopy of Transcript of Records
  • Photocopy of valid government-issued ID (back to back)
  • Original Barangay Clearance
  • Original Barangay Indigency
Important Notes

Applications are reviewed per batch by CAYDO administrators. You may re-apply after a previous application has been resolved. Submitting an application does not guarantee approval.
