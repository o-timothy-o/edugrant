SPARK

Specialized Program & Advancing Review Knowledge

One-time financial assistance for Carmona residents preparing for professional licensure and board examinations.

Who can apply?

SINAG

Scholarship, Incentives & Networking in Advancing Graduate Studies

Ongoing financial support for residents pursuing graduate or post‑graduate studies in accredited institutions.

Who can apply?

Carmona residents admitted to graduate programs who comply with academic performance and documentary requirements set by CAYDO.

CAYDO is continuously developing new scholarship and grant opportunities to reach more youth and community members in Carmona.

Carmona, a city in Cavite province under the CALABARZON region, has made youth empowerment and education a priority. Through scholarship and grant initiatives, the local government supports students and professionals in finishing their education and obtaining professional qualifications.

The Scholarship and Grant Program, administered by the Community Affairs and Youth Development Office (CAYDO), ensures that assistance is distributed in a transparent and organized manner to qualified residents.
